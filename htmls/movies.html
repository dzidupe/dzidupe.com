Pluribus Episodes 1-5

The opening to this show was incredible. Very creepy and eerie vibes. Those infected would either play dead or give others a false sense of security by smiling at them before forcing a kiss on the stranger. Showing the infected cross boundaries in such a way was an incredible way to speak on consent while also portraying what is inherently wrong about the virus that was supposed to make everyone happy.
